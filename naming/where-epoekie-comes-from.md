# Where Epoekie® comes from: the one who dwells upon | Aicent Stack

18 Oct 2026 · [Read the full article](https://blog.aicent.com/where-epoekie-comes-from/) · [Chinese version](https://blog.aicent.com/zh/where-epoekie-comes-from/)

The soul layer's name is not coined. It comes from a Greek compound meaning "the one who dwells upon", and from an ecological term for a plant that grows on a host while taking nothing from it. Entry AICENT-000 of our Where the Names Come From series.
