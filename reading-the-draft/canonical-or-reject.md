# Canonicalize or reject: one address, one spelling | Aicent Stack

17 Oct 2026 · [Read the full article](https://blog.aicent.com/canonical-or-reject/) · [Chinese version](https://blog.aicent.com/zh/canonical-or-reject/)

The address is a function of the authority string, so "two spellings of one address" is a correctness defect, not a style question. The mainstream URI ecosystem normalizes liberally; we take the opposite direction — uppercase is invalid input, not a formatting difference. Entry No. 2 of the Reading the Draft series.
