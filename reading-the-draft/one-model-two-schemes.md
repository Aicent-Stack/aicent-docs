# Why two schemes live in one document | Aicent Stack

17 Oct 2026 · [Read the full article](https://blog.aicent.com/one-model-two-schemes/) · [Chinese version](https://blog.aicent.com/zh/one-model-two-schemes/)

We registered two schemes at IANA, yet the I-D is one merged document — why one document, not two? Because the addressing model exists only once, and the two pillars' closed sets are deliberately different. Living together is not laziness; it keeps the boundary on the same page.
