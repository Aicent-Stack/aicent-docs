# What revision -01 of draft-li-rttp-iqa-addressing adds: the lease, the wire, and the answer semantics | Aicent Stack

28 Oct 2026 · [Read the full article](https://blog.aicent.com/q14-what-revision-one-adds/) · [Chinese version](https://blog.aicent.com/zh/q14-what-revision-one-adds/)

Revision -01 of draft-li-rttp-iqa-addressing was posted on 27 October 2026: 34 pages against -00's 16, with the derived-address core untouched. Reading the Draft series, No. 14: a section-by-section reading of the three new blocks — the trust lease, the two 128-octet wire formats with a byte-exact test vector, and the refusal vocabulary with its grant reference — plus the new related-work section and the rewritten security considerations. With one clarification on the record: none of it is new — the pulse frame has been public since 12 September and the envelope shipped in 1.3.1 on 4 October, twenty-three days before -01 codified it; -00 could not have carried what had not been built yet.
