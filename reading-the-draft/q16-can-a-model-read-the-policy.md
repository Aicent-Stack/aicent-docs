# Can a model read the policy? Interpretation is not verification | Aicent Stack

28 Oct 2026 · [Read the full article](https://blog.aicent.com/q16-can-a-model-read-the-policy/) · [Chinese version](https://blog.aicent.com/zh/q16-can-a-model-read-the-policy/)

The same correspondent came back within the hour: if LLMs can read natural language, maybe we don't need standards for expressing policy or even for rejection reasons. Reading the Draft series, No. 16: the experiment is welcome and the envelope already has room for it — but interpreting policy and reporting authority facts are different acts, and no reader, probabilistic or not, can argue a timestamp into the future. Auditability as replay is the line: the answer must be bytes a third party can re-derive, and in the air-gapped and store-and-forward modes the schemes are built for, there is no model to ask.
