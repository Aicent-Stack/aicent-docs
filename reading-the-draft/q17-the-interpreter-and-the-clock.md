# The interpreter and the clock: a design rule for composing both | Aicent Stack

28 Oct 2026 · [Read the full article](https://blog.aicent.com/q17-the-interpreter-and-the-clock/) · [Chinese version](https://blog.aicent.com/zh/q17-the-interpreter-and-the-clock/)

Asked to clarify the "interpreter versus clock" question, we answered with a concrete case — a mandate that permits, a lease that has expired, and two records a relying party must choose between. Reading the Draft series, No. 17: the answer both sides suspect they share is the wire record, and out of it falls a design rule — the interpreter consumes the wire answer as an input and ranks what is permitted within it; it never overrules it. Policy interpretation sorts choices among operations the authority layer has already allowed; it does not get a vote on WHETHER.
