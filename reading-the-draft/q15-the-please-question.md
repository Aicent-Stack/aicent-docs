A veteran of the object-capability tradition asked on the W3C Agent Protocol CG list where a delegator's standing policy — the "Please" — should live, and noted that nobody's system has a slot for it. Reading the Draft series, No. 15: the question is right, the gap is real, and the answer our draft gives is deliberate — the refusal vocabulary carries authority facts only, policy text stays unstandardized one layer up, and the reason a specific policy was violated remains an honest open slot nobody has filled.
