# Two answers to agent identity: AIRS and the RTTP/IQA design | Aicent Stack

29 Oct 2026 · [Read the full article](https://blog.aicent.com/rttp-iqa-vs-airs/) · [Chinese version](https://blog.aicent.com/zh/rttp-iqa-vs-airs/)

The IETF queue just gained a complete registry-based answer to agent identity — the Agent Identity Registry System (AIRS), a federated architecture with competing registrars, TPM-anchored identity tiers, and a deliberate refusal of any administrative kill switch. Set beside the two-pillar design, the two camps turn out to converge on meaningless, permanent identifiers and diverge on everything that produces them: allocation by an authority versus derivation from the authority's own bytes. This piece reads both side by side — trust topology, hardware, expiry, revocation — and lets the fork show.
