# Agent identity week: four days, four answers, and 99.1% | Aicent Stack

20 Oct 2026 · [Read the full article](https://blog.aicent.com/agent-identity-week/) · [Chinese version](https://blog.aicent.com/zh/agent-identity-week/)

Between 14 and 19 October 2026, four projects answered the same question: can an AI agent prove who it acts for? Chain of Trust (GoDaddy, Infoblox, Google, Cloudflare with Singapore's IMDA), Proof's x401, Circle's x402 pairing at Money20/20, and Vouch Protocol's Agent Trust Index &mdash; whose first sweep found 99.1% of 46,303 public MCP agents cannot present a verifiable identity. A reading of what each answer anchors trust in, what all four leave open, and where the dual-pillar answer sits.
