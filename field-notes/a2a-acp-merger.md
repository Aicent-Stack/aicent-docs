# The protocol war is over: ACP has folded into A2A, and the nameplate layer is still empty | Aicent Stack

20 Oct 2026 · [Read the full article](https://blog.aicent.com/a2a-acp-merger/) · [Chinese version](https://blog.aicent.com/zh/a2a-acp-merger/)

IBM Research launched ACP in March 2025; in August 2025 it merged into A2A under the Linux Foundation; by August 2026 both MCP and A2A sat under the same foundation. The agent protocol war ended. One year on, the tool layer and the conversation layer are consolidated and the addressing layer is still unassigned &mdash; 150+ organizations, tens of thousands of registered servers, and no canonical way to write down which agent you mean. A reading of the merger's own words, what it did not solve, and where the dual-pillar answer sits.
