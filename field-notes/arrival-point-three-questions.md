# At the arrival point: three questions, three answers | Aicent Stack

2 Nov 2026 · [Read the full article](https://blog.aicent.com/arrival-point-three-questions/) · [Chinese version](https://blog.aicent.com/zh/arrival-point-three-questions/)

On 27 October we left three questions at the door of the TPAC spatial-computing breakout — naming, freshness, and binding, all asked from the arriving device's point of view. The session has met; its summary has not yet appeared on the list. This piece does not wait for it: each question already has a worked answer running in the two-pillar stack, and each answer can be checked offline from published bytes — a name computed rather than issued, a clock carried in the answer itself, and a binding an arrival can verify without contacting anyone.
