# Four questions to the W3C AI Agent Protocol Community Group | Aicent Stack

21 Oct 2026 · [Read the full article](https://blog.aicent.com/four-questions-to-w3c-cg/) · [Chinese version](https://blog.aicent.com/zh/four-questions-to-w3c-cg/)

On 18 October 2026 we sent four open questions to public-agentprotocol@w3.org, the public list of the W3C AI Agent Protocol Community Group: preimage defense, address assurance, trust leases, and statement granularity. They are our questions but they are not about us — they are four rows of the agentic stack we could not find answered in the Community Group's technical report on Agentic Web protocols (2026-09-24), or anywhere else. The letter now stands in the public archive (No. 0009); the first substantive reply arrived three days later — a revoke-inside-the-lease-window counterexample from the author of an independent delegation-layer draft — and our answer went up the same day. This piece records the four questions and the first echo.
